CTV ads are finishing almost every view, but viewability isn't being measured

Across 4 days, the 3 CTV creatives served 41,057 impressions for $1,440 ($35.06 CPM). 99.4% of impressions ran to completion, and the quartile funnel barely leaks. Click-through is near zero (2 clicks), which is normal for CTV. Only 4 impressions carry viewability data, so viewability can't be judged yet.
