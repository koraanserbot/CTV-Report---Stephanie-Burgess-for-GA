Social CTV campaign · Delivery report

The full run: 85K impressions, and the 30s ad caught up

Across the full run the campaign delivered 85,466 CTV impressions and 85,130 completed views (99.6%). The 15s ads carried 66.2% of impressions, but the single 30s ad became the most-delivered creative at 28,855. It also completed best, at 99.66% vs 99.58% for 15s. The final day, Nov 3, added 11,548 impressions.

Why 15s leads, and why the 30s caught up

15s leads in total because there are two 15s creatives (28,322 and 28,289 impressions) against one 30s creative (28,855). Per creative, delivery is level. The 30s ad overtook the same "Burgess for Georgia" ad in 15s by 1.9%. Through Nov 1 it had trailed by 3.5%.

How the run ended

Nov 3 was the third-biggest day of the run (11,548, behind 11,766 on Nov 1 and 11,683 on Oct 29). It was the strongest day for the 30s ad, at 4,424, and it finished at 99.8% completion on both lengths. WatchFree+ carried 61.8% of the day, above its 54.0% share of the full run.

The gap is small but consistent

Both lengths hold above 99% at every milestone, which is strong for CTV. The 30s ad loses fewer viewers at 50%, 75% and 100%, and its lead at 100% is 0.08 points over the full run. The weakest finisher is "Stephanie Burgess for State Representative" 15s at 99.55%.

Where the mix differs

WatchFree+ and Telly carry both lengths. Telly is nearly 50/50 (5,380 15s vs 5,340 30s), while WTOC News is 85% 15s and the WTOC Fire TV app is 94% 15s. WTOC News also has the lowest completion of the big channels at 98.8%.
