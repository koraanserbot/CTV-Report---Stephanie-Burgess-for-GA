Social CTV campaign · Delivery report

15s ads win on reach, 30s ads finish a touch better

The campaign delivered 63,269 CTV impressions and 62,968 completed views (99.5%). The 15s ads carried 67.4% of impressions, but the 30s ad completed at 99.62% vs 99.48% for 15s. WatchFree+ alone delivered 49% of all impressions.

Why 15s leads

It is the number of ads, not a preference for short ones. There are two 15s creatives (21,323 and 21,350 impressions) and one 30s creative (20,596). Each creative got about the same delivery. For the same "Burgess for Georgia" ad, 15s led 30s by only 3.5%.

Where the mix differs

WatchFree+ and Telly carry both lengths. Telly is nearly 50/50 (4,408 15s vs 4,335 30s), while WTOC News is 85% 15s and the WTOC Fire TV app is 94% 15s. WTOC News also has the lowest completion of the big channels at 98.8%.

CTV by Instagram or Facebook
This split is not possible with the current data

Nothing in the dataset identifies Instagram or Facebook traffic. I checked the app name, site, seller domain, publisher name and campaign columns. None contain "Instagram" or "Facebook". The only social reference is the campaign name, "Social CTV", which describes the whole campaign.
